🎉 **Big News!**

We’re thrilled to announce that _Helixque_ has been selected for the **Vercel OSS Program, Fall Cohort ’25**.

- 🤝 **Closer collaboration** with Vercel on performance and DX (Developer Experience)
- ⚙️ **Better deployment workflows** and preview environments for our contributors
- 🚢 **Faster shipping** of new features and updates to the community
